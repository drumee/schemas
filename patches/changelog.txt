  WHAT AN EXPIRED REWARD IS. A gift that ends, not an unpaid invoice. The
  allowance returns to free, existing files STAY -- readable, downloadable,

  the limit. Nothing here ever deletes anything. That is a deliberate departure
  from offline/subscription/downgrade.js, which for a LAPSED PAID PLAN escalates
  over ~181 days and then calls clean() to entity_delete every hub and wipe the
  desk. Sharing that ladder would mean destroying data we gave away as a prize.

  NOTHING IN THIS ENTRY IS LOAD-BEARING. reward_expiry_due is query-only and the
  worker only sends mail. If neither is ever installed, the term still ends on
  the right day and enforcement still holds; users are simply not warned. A
  notification feature must not be able to break the entitlement it describes.

  reward_expiry_due answers "who, at which stage", and excludes users UNDER the
  free allowance entirely -- for them expiry changes a number and nothing else,
  and with ~100 people in the campaign, mailing the unaffected is expensive
  noise. Usage is the owner's own total (hubs owned + own desk from
  yp.disk_usage), the same pair my_disk_limit adds up and the same one
  enforcement measures, so "over quota" means here what it means everywhere. The
  allowance is READ from the seeded free row rather than written as 5000000000 --
  the free plan has already moved once (20 GB -> 5 GB) and a literal would
  quietly mis-select the whole population next time.

  day the term ends. `stage` is the most urgent threshold the user has REACHED,

  the most urgent reached stage also makes catch-up correct: a user first seen at
  5 days out gets the 7-day notice, not a "30 days remaining" mail that is
  already false, and the worker records the skipped stages as superseded so they
  cannot fire later.
